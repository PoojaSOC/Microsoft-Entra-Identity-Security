A new employee, Daniel Silva, joins Contoso Professional Services as a Cybersecurity Analyst in the Security Operations team.

Business Objectives
Create a Microsoft Entra ID user account
Assign Microsoft 365 licensing
Configure Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Apply Conditional Access policies
Provide Teams and SharePoint access
Enable OneDrive for Business
Grant Security Reader access
Follow least-privilege principles

Expected Outcome
Daniel can access the business resources required for his role while administrative permissions remain restricted.


Security Requirements
No Global Administrator access
Access assigned through groups where possible
Emergency access account excluded from Conditional Access
Auditability of identity changes
