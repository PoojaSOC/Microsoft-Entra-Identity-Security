## Business Scenario
Daniel Silva joins Contoso Professional Services as a Cybersecurity Analyst in the Security Operations team.
## Business Objectives
- Create a Microsoft Entra ID account.
- Assign Microsoft 365 licensing.
- Configure MFA and Conditional Access.
- Provide Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive access.
- Grant read-only Security Reader access.
## Security Requirements
- Follow least-privilege principles.
- No Global Administrator access for Daniel.
- Manage access through groups where possible.
- Exclude emergency access from Conditional Access policies.
- Maintain audit records of identity changes.
## Expected Outcome
Daniel receives the access required for his role without unnecessary administrative permissions.
